AC - Aflibercept

  • Type: indication
    Condition Relation: ALL
    Aflibercept indication criteria
    Aflibercept may be indicated when ALL of the following are present:
    • Type: indication
      Condition Relation: ANY
      Clinical diagnosis required
      Clinical diagnosis of 1 or more of the following:
      • Diabetic macular edema
        Diabetic macular edema
        Question: Is there documentation of diabetic macular edema in the patient's medical record?
      • Diabetic retinopathy
        Diabetic retinopathy
        Question: Is there documentation of diabetic retinopathy in the patient's medical record?
      • Macular edema post-occlusion
        Macular edema following central or branch retinal vein occlusion
        Question: Is there documentation of macular edema following central or branch retinal vein occlusion in the patient's medical record?
      • Metastatic colorectal cancer
        Metastatic colorectal cancer with progression of disease on initial therapy
        Question: Is there documentation of metastatic colorectal cancer with progression of disease on initial therapy in the patient's medical record?
      • Neovascular macular degeneration
        Neovascular (wet, or exudative) age-related macular degeneration
        Question: Is there documentation of neovascular (wet, or exudative) age-related macular degeneration in the patient's medical record?
      • Retinopathy of prematurity
        Retinopathy of prematurity
        Question: Is there documentation of retinopathy of prematurity in the patient's medical record?
    • No active inflammation
      No active intraocular inflammation
      Question: Is there documentation in the medical record indicating no active intraocular inflammation?
    • No concurrent infection
      No concurrent ocular or periocular infection
      Question: Is there documentation in the medical record indicating no concurrent ocular or periocular infection?
